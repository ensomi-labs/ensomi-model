R2 generator architecture

R2 writes a 4K osu!mania chart one head row at a time. It is told when the rows are (the head skeleton), the BPM grid and the song length. It gets no audio and no release times. At each head row it picks what all four lanes do and, for lanes holding a long note, where in the gap before that row the note ends. Requests for LN share or difficulty apply over song-time scopes, and a locality rule decides which decisions may read them.

1At a glance

  • The unit is a head rowK head rows give K + 1 decisions. Decision k decides the LN releases in (tk−1, tk] and the new taps and LN heads at tk. A final EOS decision, fixed at T, only closes what is still held.
  • Rows are chosen jointlyEach lane takes one of 5 codes, so a row is one of 5⁴ = 625 actions, scored together by a low-rank joint head and masked to legal ones.
  • Releases come from a pointerRelease times are picked from a finite set of grid-snapped candidates in the gap, lane by lane, averaged over both mirror orientations.
  • Memory is a causal TCNDilated convolutions over committed rows see the last 511 rows. A landmark read attends to every 64th row. The TCN holds 83% of the weights.
  • Conditions are scopedLN share and difficulty (tiled star) targets apply over [a, b) in song time. They enter by FiLM, only for decisions that rule L lets read them.
  • Training is teacher-forced CE256-decision windows from ranked and loved 2–6★ charts, with condition tracks drawn from each chart's own measured values. Condition terms score only decisions that can see them.

3The decision unit

Decision k belongs to head row k. It decides every LN release in (tk−1, tk], on the row or inside the gap, together with the new taps and LN heads at tk. Inside the model this happens in two steps. One joint choice over the four lane codes (625 actions) fixes, for each held lane, whether it keeps holding, releases on the row or releases in the gap, and for each lane whether a tap or LN head starts at tk. Then the release time of each lane that releases in the gap is picked from the candidates, lane by lane (§4). Decision 0 has no gap. The EOS decision sits at the song length T, which is given: its row action is forced, and only the release times of lanes still held are chosen. With nothing held it outputs nothing. Every row must place at least one head. common.py:7-18, state.py:49-101, sampling.py:84-105

4Release times

A release is never a free real number. For a gap (a, b) of at least 2 ms, the candidates are the 1/32-beat and 1/24-beat grid positions strictly inside it, the midpoint, and the points 1/8 and 1/4 beat before the next row, each rounded to the millisecond. The midpoint guarantees the set is never empty. In training, source releases snap to the nearest candidate, and a release exactly on the next row becomes code 1. candidates.py:58-118, cache.py:121-133

Several lanes closing in one gap

A chord can release together or apart. The lanes that close in a gap are scored one after another as directed factors. Each factor sees where earlier releases of the same decision landed, and which lanes release on the row, through 28 relation features per candidate (4 lane slots × 7). The order is lane 0 → 3 in one orientation and 3 → 0 in the mirrored one, and the release likelihood is the average of the two orientations:

log Q = logsumexp( Σ log q_fwd , Σ log q_mirrored ) − log 2 (0 when nothing closes in the gap)

That makes a decision's probability exactly mirror-equivariant. Sampling flips a fair coin for the orientation, then samples lane by lane in its order. model.py:289-309, sampling.py:93-105, tests/r2/test_mirror.py

5The network

One forward pass scores a window of decisions in teacher forcing; generation runs the same modules one decision at a time with a cached TCN. The two hands are processed in parallel with shared weights: hand 0 lists lanes 0, 1, 2, 3 and hand 1 lists 3, 2, 1, 0, so mirroring a chart swaps the hands. model.py:226-287, common.py:3-5

6Requests, scopes and rule L

A request

  • A scope [a, b) in song ms, 0 ≤ a < b ≤ T.
  • At most one target per property: LN share in [0, 1], difficulty as tiled star over a scope of at least 30 s, or both on the same scope.
  • Strength fixed at the default. Ranges are refused.
  • Style, demand, priority and transitions must be empty; they are reserved and raise when set.

request_set.py:34-121

The request set refuses

  • a request added at or after its own start (the committed frontier must be before a);
  • two targets for the same property with overlapping scopes;
  • withdrawing or replacing a request once its scope has started.

Requests for different properties may also have different scopes that partly overlap; the record lists them as co-active. Flags mark a scope with no head row, an undefined readout, or a length outside what training covered (LN 8–512 beats or whole song; difficulty 30/60/120 s or whole song).

request_set.py:128-259

Properties are measured one way everywhere

One module, ν = r2-nu-v1, defines both properties for labels, condition frames, validation and readouts. An object belongs to the scope of its head. LN share is LN heads over heads in the scope. Difficulty is tiled star: the scope's own objects are repeated until they fill 240 s, then scored with the 2024-10-07 mania star rating, because that rating depends on length. It is undefined under 30 s. properties.py:36-188

Rule L: a decision reads a scope only if all it can produce lies inside it

Decision k produces its gap closes and its row together. If k could close a hold before a, or produce anything at or after b, a request on [a, b) would leak outside its scope. Masking single candidates cannot fix that, because the pointer normalises over all of them. So decision k reads interval [a, b) only when its row time is inside and, if a lane is held entering k, its earliest release candidate is at or after a. EOS reads only when every candidate it has is inside. locality.py:1-47

7Generation, step by step

  1. Refuse a baseline style; check the chart seed by replay; check that every request starts after the committed frontier.
  2. Build the effective track: one interval per target, with provenance and flags.
  3. Run the TCN over the seed's history tokens and keep its cache, saving a landmark every 64 rows.
  4. For each decision k: derive held lanes and clocks, read the cache and landmarks, compute query features, find V_k by rule L, build the frames, and get the hand vectors z.
  5. Score the 625 row actions, mask illegal ones, and sample with the Gumbel-max trick at temperature 1 (CPU float64, no truncation).
  6. Flip a fair coin for the orientation. For each lane that closes in the gap, in that order, score every candidate with the current placements and sample one.
  7. Commit; append history token k to the TCN cache (EOS never becomes a token).
  8. After EOS: replay to hit objects, measure every target with ν, record masking and defects (holds ≤ 60 ms, releases 1–40 ms before another lane's head), and write the record.
  9. Export .osu only if the chart is complete and the legality check passes.

sampling.py:46-121, generate.py:59-136

Default operating point. "Default strength" in the formulation means the generator the frozen selection rule picks. Its hash covers the training recipe, the selection rule and its source, the decoding, and the conditioning settings. Every generation record names it. operating_point.py:20-65

The selection rule (post-run, select.py:42-136): among regular checkpoints after warm-up with full panels, keep those that pass the guards: legal output, natural LN share within ±0.05 of the source, LN onset slope ≥ 0.7 with MAE ≤ 0.15, own-history calibration gap ≤ 0.05, short holds ≤ 0.5%, short releases no more than the source, identical output after a scope's exit. Then pick the earliest within 2 SE of the lowest natural NLL (averaged over three checkpoints, song-group bootstrap). If none passes, nothing is selected.

8Training

Data: ranked and loved 2–6★ charts of the fit split, cached one .npz per chart (head times, codes, snapped gap releases, grid, song length), round-trip checked. Song groups split into fit_train and fit_dev by a hash. splits.py:1-60, cache.py:73-246

Loss

ℓ_j = −( log P(A_j) + log Q(U_j) ) per scored decision j L_base = (1 / N̄) Σ_j w_j ℓ_j w_j = u if V_j is empty, else 1 L_ln = (1 / N̄_ln) Σ over decisions reading an LN interval of −log P(tap-vs-LN split | heads, release types) L_star = (1 / N̄_star) Σ over decisions reading a difficulty interval of ℓ_j L = L_base + λ_ln L_ln + λ_star L_star

The divisors are fixed expected counts per batch, measured by draw_sim, so each window can be backpropagated alone and the batch gradient is still exact. The LN term scores only the tap-versus-LN part of the row: the probability of the chosen action among legal actions with the same head mask and release types. The trainer refuses to start until N̄ values matching the draw are in the config; both v2 configs have them null. loss.py:1-68, model.py:384-411, train_ce.py:313-319

Draw parameters (A1 defaults)

  • LN candidates: a fresh partition into 8/16/32/64-beat pieces; whole song with p 0.10; otherwise runs of 2–8 pieces with p 0.25. Value = the source's own LN share there.
  • Difficulty candidates: one cell length (30/60/120 s) and phase (0/10/20 s), or whole song with p 0.10. Values from the label file.
  • Aligned start with p 0.6: lead 0–32 decisions before an onset; LN candidates whose value differs from the previous 64 rows by z ≥ 2 get weight 3.
  • 1–3 intervals per kind in the window, consecutive with p 0.5.

What v1 showed that v2 does not yet address in training

  • Exposure bias. Teacher-forced LN forecasts are calibrated, but on the model's own histories they sit 0.129 ± 0.044 higher (v1 at 61.4M exposures); natural LN share from the start runs 0.10–0.13 above the source. v2 measures this gap; it does not train against it.
  • Star has no effect. The requested star changed nothing (KL 2e-6 nats per row over three checkpoints), and the given head rows already explain most of it (R² 0.75). v2's answer is the residual against b(S) plus the stage-2 own-sample term.
  • The landmark memory is unused. Removing its read improved action NLL at 39.49M. The research notes' reading is that CE on real histories gives no pressure to use it.
  • Variation is uncommitted. Variance across random seeds on one skeleton was twice the variance across skeletons, and LN share oscillated between checkpoints.

Code-level observations to check

  • The relaxed-proxy and true-star samples draw their seeds from the training draw generator, so turning the term on changes the window sequence. train_ce.py:473, 496
  • With star_conditions: auto, star conditions switch off silently when the v2 label file is missing, and A1's λ_star then does nothing. train_ce.py:215-218
  • A0 applies dropout before choosing intervals per song; v1 dropped from the chosen track. The plan does not say which. conditions.py:258-278
  • In-run records hash the operating point without the recipe, so their hash differs from the one selection writes. evaluate.py:371
  • The proxy samples a whole difficulty scope inside one training step; for whole-song scopes (p 0.10) that cost is unmeasured.
