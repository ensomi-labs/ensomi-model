# Broader learning, scope accounting and release support

This phase tested whether the [formal LN/proposal diagnosis](ln-feedback-and-proposal-diagnosis.md)
could be repaired by learning the actual R1 decision owners, exposing missing
control state and recovering real release support. It produced useful partial
gains, but no qualified model.

## Full R1 learned routing and lost quantity calibration

Ordinary full-R1 learning trained 2,753,715 parameters while audio/H/R stayed
frozen. A matched missing-history view improved its own likelihood but worsened
both Stream seeds' pressure versus ordinary fitting. The broader 512-update
continuation then used 1,024 new windows from 748 charts/651 groups, with native
panel audios excluded from this phase. Source NLL improved 1.618333→1.572470.

Four Stream cases' maximum consecutive-H column ages fell 21/16/8/17→8/7/7/6;
inspected TAP-only movement improved, so not all gain came from LN occupancy
blocking a finger. But three new LN-amount failures and an override-difficulty
failure appeared. Blizzard still lacked the inspected anchor-plus-TAP relation.
The [full-row account](/Users/l/projects/ensomi-model/docs/research/full_row_learning_and_difficulty_response.md)
at `6fa3a54` preserves the gain and rejection.

Reducing only neural difficulty code lowered some achieved stars while increasing
repeated-head fraction in all four tested cases. Combining the lower code with
the broader model yielded zero attack excess yet new isolated 12/16-head runs.
Real ranked sixteen-head anchors with accompaniment prevent turning run length
into a universal ban. The reusable recurrence observer consequently retains
actual span, cadence and companion activity, later including continuing holds.

## Scope progress was observable and only partly useful

`c0db49c` added factual LN/head accounting per declared and effective-owner scope.
It demonstrated two histories with identical finite row context and exact replay
but different older LN allocation. That proves an information gap, not its causal
share of native error. The adapter preserves unknown-LN outputs exactly and
does not impose a uniform prefix quota.

On six known-LN outputs, mean fraction error improved .153796→.099117;
the matched context-only adapter reached .114877. The extra .015759 gain missed
the .02 comparator criterion, and override amount remained near .897 for .6.
The 512 training programs contained no overlapping LN requests; only 28 of
11,398 late known-LN queries had >.2 cumulative amount error. Correct state
plumbing did not supply the missing recovery situations. [Allocation study](/Users/l/projects/ensomi-model/docs/research/scoped_ln_allocation.md).

A new STYX witness repeated one finger while the other three remained held,
despite near-zero attack excess. Keeping-hold/release alternatives remained
supported. The observer had to retain actual entering holds before interpreting
zero companion heads as free fingers. Ranked LN-jack contrasts also contained
valid 38/40/42/44/46/48-ms tails excluded by the then-50-ms support.

## Relaxed support and joint fitting did not qualify

Restoring HH/RH/HR support to 60/25/21 ms was compared separately from learning.
Matched R/R1 and full audio/H/R/R1 fits preserved source identities and complete
native evaluation. R/R1 improved six-case LN MAE to .061129; joint fitting
improved several NLLs. Neither achieved the .10 difficulty improvement, and
they introduced four/three previously passing numerical failures respectively.
On fixed H, R/R1 produced a 21-head concentrated run where the profile-only
comparator's maximum was seven. [Release-support learning](/Users/l/projects/ensomi-model/docs/research/release_support_joint_learning.md),
`784ac6f`.

`dcc21bd` supplied a mandatory-H lower bound for the declared whole-chart star
algorithm. It can establish impossible difficulty under a given required H
sequence, unlike a merely low-scoring sampled construction. Fewer total H did
not guarantee a lower bound: one D2 case fell 1,899→1,399 H while its bound
rose 2.6750→2.6855 and actual stars rose 3.3759→4.1993. Timing distribution
and R1's remaining excess must be considered separately.

The recipe audit also found high-LN source exposure at 33.90% versus a natural
equal-song/chart 6.09%; hidden-LN examples were still 30.41% high-LN. Independent
dropout did not undo balancing. This is a measured training-distribution change,
not a proven exclusive explanation for fragmented tails. Missing-condition
behavior and explicitly requested rare styles require distinct coverage.

## A proven H interaction limit still did not yield a useful fit

The bounded H base combined projected control and audio additively before an
affine readout. It could shift logits but not directly reselect audio features
by condition on that path; the residual still supplied other interactions.
`de5d560` added multiplicative scoped audio interaction and compared 512 H-only
updates on the same 1,024 draws. Audio/R/R1 remained frozen.

Both H likelihoods improved. D2 MAE worsened from 1.38534 to 1.61354/1.78828,
and three generated H plans had lower bounds above three stars, excluding even
2±1 for every R1 realization. Modulation's better D4/LN means did not offset
failed D2/D6 and restored-scope criteria. [Matched H study](/Users/l/projects/ensomi-model/docs/research/head_audio_control_interaction.md).

not merely shorter tails. It asked for player/mapper reasoning, real corpus
comparison and a presently failing evaluation before architectural retraining.
