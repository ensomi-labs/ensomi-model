### 1.2 Provenance judgement

Every kept entry is labelled `human`. Basis (details in the header of `verbatim.md`):

1. Interactive Desktop thread: `originator = Codex Desktop`, `source = vscode`, `thread_source = user`; message is a `UserMessage` event. 211 of 218 such events in the scan carry a UI client id; the 7 without one are all in the main session, mid-turn steering from the same person (same voice, topic and timing).
2. Excluded as not human: `codex exec` sessions (all 2026-09-30, matching `.sync/cp/jobs/*/brief.md`: `agent-brief`), guardian-review and spawned subagent prompts, automatic goal continuation text, environment/AGENTS/plugin injections, ambient app-context blocks.
3. Human but not free typing, flagged in `verbatim.md`: answers to agent questions (`answer`), comments attached to a selected span of an agent reply (`annotation`; the selected span is agent text), and goal-text edits reported by the app (`goal-edit`). Two pasted expert replies (V25, V59) are external model text relayed by the human and are not counted as the human's technical direction.
4. Confidence: high. The logs cannot exclude a script driving the UI, but style, timing, mixed-language typing and the absence of any matching machine brief make it very unlikely. Direct human statements of intuition or hypothesis are marked as such below (V55, V71, V80).
