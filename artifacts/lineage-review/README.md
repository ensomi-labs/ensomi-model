## Preliminary observations (main thread, before any reviewer result)

<a id="o-response-undefined"></a>**Observation, checked against `5c56e28:docs/formulation/gameplay-state.md` and `notation.md`, 2026-09-30.** Three things the formulation says, which bear on how to read the lineage:

- The target response is named and left undefined: its quantities, scales and comparison rules are to be set from mapper evidence after the initial annotated dataset is complete ("Target response and frontier", "Defining the response from mapper evidence"). Every frontier, response or player-state object the lineage built was therefore fitted to proxies without a specified target. Whether the annotated dataset was ever completed was not checked.
- The canonical profile excludes individual capacity, fatigue and physiology ("Canonical gameplay profile"). The feedback index records later requests for per-finger strain with decay calibrated on hand physiology (problem G). These differ in scope. Which one the player response state of key 1 means is the human's to say.
- The contract allows joint reasoning over provisional future rows with prefix commit, and internal beat coordinates (`notation.md`, "Chart object and time", "Generation and optional controls"). Strict next-event sampling on a millisecond clock is an implementation choice of the lineage, narrower than the contract.

<a id="o-pilot-scale"></a>**Observation, doc-claim only, 2026-09-30.** Read in Codex's tagged notes (`relay-notes-audio-joint-2026-09:artifacts/clean-joint-and-current-state.md`), not checked against logs:

- The three-arm clean joint comparison trained 4.68M-parameter models for 4,096 updates on 8,192 windows from 3,736 charts (16,957 s in total).
- The from-scratch ordinary expert was a 512-update fit of 235.73 s on sixteen four-second units from four songs.
- In that expert's native output, head times came in adjacent pairs of 10 ms or less that the sources do not contain, and 155 of 191 extreme short LN tails ended at the immediately following head.

<a id="h-scale-and-grid"></a>**Hypothesis, open, 2026-09-30.** Two readings these numbers suggest, neither tested: (a) the late "architecture" conclusions rest on pilots too small to say anything about architecture; (b) a share of the fragmented-LN complaint is a timing-representation defect (near-duplicate head times from a millisecond hazard with no grid or minimum gap), not a row-model or response defect. Slices 05 and 03 are the ones that would confirm or reject them. A reviewer given these readings tends to confirm them; the slice questions are written neutrally for that reason.
