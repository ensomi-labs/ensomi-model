The early interventions were reasonable diagnostics of narrow mechanisms. The Good Luck upstream chord makes a future repeat unavoidable; a different later state already assigns 94.08% to the safe column.
These do not support a single inference such as “the scorer cannot see recovery” or “better candidates never exist.” The first concerns commitment consequences and residual scoring; the second concerns stochastic tail mass (`checked-artifact`: `20260924-feasible-release-v1/head-plan-crossover/frontier-audit/result.json`, three states).
The source census is particularly useful because it preserves short cross-column structures and rare ranked short LNs instead of silently removing counterexamples (`checked-artifact`).

The response-state fit is a real negative result for **that 384-update recipe**, not for persistent state as an architecture.
The response objective improves the training-bank probe and beats continued CE on the native selected cost, but both fitted endpoints regress relative to their parent.
All four selected Stream bank songs already had zero baseline excess; this sharply limits the claim that the run adequately trained the subsequently observed Stream failure (`doc-claim`: `player_state_conditioning.md:140`, `:264`).
Adding the state, changing weights, source gradients, sampled-future gradients and finite coverage are entangled; no repeated fitting seeds quantify optimizer variance.

The later action-response attempt better separates what is being represented from what is accepted. Its state can distinguish two histories even when both pass the acceptance budget.
