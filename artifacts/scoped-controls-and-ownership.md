# Scoped controls and the correction of decision ownership

The human made difficulty, semantic style and LN/TAP amount explicit inputs
that can change over a bounded future range. Controls are approximate outcome
requests; their effective ranges must remain separate in evaluation. Existing
rows and crossing holds survive a change. Sources: [control request, private,
local](private/human-inputs/01a0ebe8-d368-7ba3-9d56-48da0524c5b6.md#m0417)
and [scope/quality clarification](private/human-inputs/01a0ebe8-d368-7ba3-9d56-48da0524c5b6.md#m0453).

## Ranked grounding and the typed-resource detour

The 8,774-chart ranked 2–6★ audit found no sub-37-ms same-column attack in
about 12.92M intervals. That is corpus support evidence, not an inferred
human capability threshold. HH, hold duration (HR) and release-to-head (RH)
are distinct relationships. The implementation tried shared recovery support
and scoped conditions in a typed resource planner (`bcb6f3a`, `7329b0e`,
`ca3dc65` onward). Its [own account](/Users/l/projects/ensomi-model/docs/research/typed_audio_continuation.md)
preserves successive fits, amount feedback, guidance and broader qualification.

This prototype moved counts/types and release identities into the skeleton,
leaving R1 to assign columns. An independent H clock reduced selected star MAE
from 1.235 to .979 but increased <=40-ms LN prevalence from 4.28% to 7.33%.
Fixed-program column reassignment improved many recovery relationships while
leaving every short lifetime unchanged. That localized a decision-ownership
limit: a downstream row scorer cannot move a tail already fixed upstream.
The [scoped demand/frontier analysis](/Users/l/projects/ensomi-model/docs/research/scoped_demand_frontier.md)
at `24096a1` explains this evidence and the separate local LN-probability bound.

The typed implementation also failed to implement an inherited bounded-timing
configuration as its name suggested. Repairing that mismatch and removing a
bounded binomial restriction restored expressive local preferences, but neither
was a proof of quality. A scoped high LN proportion must still permit a nearly
certain local TAP passage; positive mathematical support alone is insufficient.

## Quantity control improved before arrangement control

Audio-conditioned demand (`57cdbbf`) learned an expected head-activity reference.
A 2,000-update fit reduced 24-window head-count MAE from 31.26 to 13.77.
Combining it with stronger 60/50/50-ms recovery improved some low-LN difficulty
cells, but high-LN calibration and articulation remained weaker. Absence of
<=40-ms holds under that support is a filtering fact, not learned quality.

Independent per-attribute scope encoding (`7c122d4`) used the same 16,000 draws
and improved median override difficulty-proxy error from 1.159 to .639 on
eight audios. The range still varied substantially; one five-star/high-LN
cell worsened. A Take peak had five repeated TAP quads despite acceptable
whole-star deviation. The [demand study](/Users/l/projects/ensomi-model/docs/research/audio_demand_balance.md)
records both calibration gains and that grouping counterexample.

The human then explicitly corrected the responsibility split: chord quantity
and arrangement belong to R1, with relevant control information supplied there.
The skeleton should not absorb those responsibilities to work around weak row
choices. See [correction, private,
local](private/human-inputs/01a0ebe8-d368-7ba3-9d56-48da0524c5b6.md#m0509)
and [broader design constraint](private/human-inputs/01a0ebe8-d368-7ba3-9d56-48da0524c5b6.md#m0512).

## Complete-row ownership restored

`c5b7db8` restores H as a head-bearing timestamp owner, R as pure-release
timing, and R1 as the complete-row owner: cardinality, columns, TAP/LN types,
and which holds close, including closures at H. All receive applicable controls
and audio. R1 receives timing preview and its own factual row history.
`bbd1a7f` passes feasible release windows back to timing; strict preference
isolation therefore does not imply identical effective timing under every
change in TAP history.

The [controlled model](/Users/l/projects/ensomi-model/docs/research/controlled_audio_continuation.md)
normalizes layout within count families, then applies the candidate consequence
energy before normalization across complete rows. Otherwise a shared group cost
can cancel and fail to penalize a costly singleton layout family. This is a
probability/ownership property, not proof that `frontier2` is a calibrated
player-response model.

At the 2,500-update endpoint, projected integral LN feedback improved tested
whole-song amount error from .0548 to .0017 for requested .2 and from .1185
to .0089 for requested .7. Star errors remained about 1.06 and 1.44. The
controller improved quantity but added a prefix-balance preference that was
later challenged in [the LN-feedback investigation](ln-feedback-and-proposal-diagnosis.md).

Giving this row model valid reference H did not eliminate error. A 2.999-star
mostly-single source became 4.852 stars with the same 783 H and 498 extra
heads. A slower-chord case and LN case behaved differently, preventing a
single allocation of blame to H or R1. A bounded count-prior comparison
improved this singles case by .312 MAE but missed its .4 progress and .6
absolute-error criteria; it was not selected.

## What these iterations leave reusable

Keep independent scope semantics, exact replay of crossing holds, timing-only
preview and complete-row choice. Preserve separate actual control ranges and
the distinction between whole-chart official star computation and local strain
proxies. The later fresh ordinary recipe deliberately questions using local
strain labels for every quiet/dense crop; that is a substantive training choice,
not a contradiction to erase from history.

Do not equate a trained demand predictor, packaged bundle, small LN amount
error, stricter recovery support or fast execution with ordinary musical
organization. The next [conditional-learning studies](conditional-learning.md)
ask whether the supplied request actually changes the right choices on factual
and generated histories. The eventual [clean joint comparison](clean-joint-and-current-state.md)
revisits audio/H/R/R1 together rather than continuing frozen components forever.
