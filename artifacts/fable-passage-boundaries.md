# Passage boundaries and the comparison design

Written 2026-10-03 by a fresh Fable subagent for main session `4259953e`. Labels: **[code]** read in the committed source, **[notes]** read in the research notes, **[measured]** computed by the two jobs in the provenance line, **[estimate]** neither computed nor cited, **[reasoning]** my argument, **(proposed)** my proposal. Notes paths are relative to `.git/research-relay/notes/`, code paths to the repository root at `2044c2b`.

## Answer in one paragraph

Bars should stay as the alignment of passage boundaries and the unit of attribution; a fixed count of bars should not stay as the measure of a passage's length. The placeholder (four canonical bars inside one musical segment, trailing block dropped) never scores 6.1% of all fit-split heads and more than 10% of the heads in one chart in six **[measured]**, and in a ritardando chain four "global bars" can be four red lines a second apart. I propose a passage as a span of the condition's grid, never of the output's events: musical segments join into *key segments* while neighbours' canonical BPM stays within a stated tolerance; inside a key segment passages are nominally 16 canonical beats cut at the nearest bar start, every remainder kept, every event in exactly one passage; each passage carries its actual length in beats and seconds and its context availability as covariates of the reference; and the song statistic weights passages by duration in seconds, so moving a cut by a bar or splitting off a tail changes the verdict continuously, not by a cliff. The reference for a passage is keyed by requested star, the passage's canonical BPM, the condition's fixed aspects and the scope shape, continuous where it can be, and never by the song's passage count, notated tempo, realised star or ranked/loved status. The song null is empirical and dependence-aware but not stratified by exact passage count: whole fit songs smoothed in (log duration, star) for whole-song scopes, same-shape windows cut from fit songs at every position for partial scopes, and a modelled 1/duration variance law where the corpus runs out, each checked on calibration by band. The fold-per-segment decision has one consequence no segmentation can hide, the same music at 158 and 162 BPM is described in different vocabularies against different references; that is honest while both references exist, and one pre-registered test says whether it costs calibration.

## 1. What is established

- Each musical segment folds into [80, 160) by its own power of two; beats restart at every segment offset; bars are a separate list of bar starts holding `meter` canonical beats **[code]** (`beats.py:11-13, 34-43`). A canonical 4/4 bar lasts 1.5 s at 160 and 3.0 s at 80; the placeholder's passages span 6.2 to 11.7 s in the fit split (5th to 95th percentile of each chart's shortest) **[measured]**.
- A bar start within 2 ms of a whole canonical beat is put on it; a bar cut short by the next bar start counts as a bar unless the remainder is within 2 ms **[code]** (`beats.py:138-158`); `time_of_bar` extrapolates before the first bar start (`beats.py:209-210`).
- Musical and redundant lines start bars unless they carry omit-first-barline; expressive lines are out of the grid **[code]** (`redlines.py:234-242`). A redundant line is on a whole canonical beat of the grid in force, possibly mid-bar, so it restarts the bar phase.
- `Spans.bars` turns global bar indices into ms spans; objects belong to spans by head time, a release with its head **[code]** (`case.py:18-19, 88`); scopes are free ms spans ([d-partial-scope](evaluation-first.md#d-partial-scope)).
- Reference keyed by the requested star, a passage compared with corpus passages of the same length from charts of that star ([d-star-key](operator-properties.md#d-star-key)); no hard cut-offs ([d-no-cutoffs](operator-properties.md#d-no-cutoffs)); one population ([d-loved-population](evaluation-first.md#d-loved-population)); the placeholder chain of surprisal, cross-fitted rank, Fisher per song against fit songs of the same key and passage count ([fable-evaluator-blockers](fable-evaluator-blockers.md) Q1, [a-fable-review](evaluation-first.md#a-fable-review)) **[notes]**.
- Fit split as measured: 17,106 ranked or loved charts, 25,485,950 heads; song duration (first head to last event) 32 s at the 1st percentile, 60 s at the 5th, 126 s median, 290 s at the 95th, 395 s at the 99th **[measured]**.

## 3. Alternatives to fixed bar counts, judged against the cases

- **Fixed count of bars (placeholder).** Fails B1, B3, B4, B5, B13 by dropping heads: 1,552,507 heads (6.1%) never scored; 2,863 charts (16.7%) lose over 10%, 796 (4.7%) over 25%; passage counts fall to 0.92 of the global-bar count at the median and 0.62 at the 5th percentile **[measured]**. Rejected as the measure of length; kept as the alignment.
- **Bar counts adapting to duration** (two bars at canonical 80, four at 159). Fixes none of the boundary rows and adds a key-dependent window; the owner accepted the 6 to 12 s spread in choosing bars for both clocks ([d-passage-bars](evaluation-first.md#d-passage-bars)). Rejected; revisit only if E5 shows load families calibrating differently by BPM band.
- **Canonical-beat windows ignoring bar lines.** Immune to B1, B2, B3, B8; passage count close to the bar tiling's (median 14 against 15) **[measured]**. But passage starts leave the downbeat, so beat phase inside a passage varies and bar-scale recurrence loses its frame; the owner chose bars. Rejected as primary; the fallback when no bar start is in reach.
- **Fixed-seconds windows.** A time-stretch changes the beats a window holds, against [p-op-clocks](operator-properties.md#p-op-clocks); the owner declined seconds for load. Rejected; one diagnostic survives (E5 by BPM band).
- **Sliding windows scored per bar with context.** Removes the phase arbitrariness (a straddling defect is seen whole by some window), handles B12 at bar resolution, reads B11 naturally; costs four times the descriptions and strong serial dependence, which an empirical by-song null absorbs. It is the stride-1 case of the proposal; E6 decides.
- **Multiscale windows.** Adopted as a family parameter: the context a family reads is in bars; the scored atom stays the passage, so one tiling and one null.
- **Event-count windows.** Boundaries then depend on the output: fewer notes, longer windows, sparsity disguised. Rejected on a principle to freeze: a passage is a function of the condition's grid and the scope, never of the generated events.
- **Musical phrases.** From the chart's notes: output-dependent. From audio: the timing work's ([d-placement-is-timing](evaluation-first.md#d-placement-is-timing)). From a skeleton: legitimate, later.

**Two clocks, one segmentation [reasoning].** The seconds clock enters a bar-aligned passage through the key: canonical BPM and length in beats fix the duration. A second segmentation for load is needed only if the spread across keys (6 to 12 s) or within a song at a fold jump damages load calibration; E5 by BPM band is the decision rule. Invariance: a tiling in canonical beats aligned to bar starts is unchanged under whole and per-segment renotation (identical coordinates on all 22,066 charts, [s-m0-m1](evaluation-first.md#s-m0-m1)), shifts with chart and grid, and under a time-stretch keeps its beat edges while its second edges scale, the signed behaviour [p-op-clocks](operator-properties.md#p-op-clocks) asks for.

## 4. Proposal: what a passage is <a id="p-passage"></a>

(proposed) A passage is defined by the case's grid and scope alone.

1. **Tiled span.** From the first bar start at or before the first event the case knows of (the chart's, or the condition's skeleton or context event, whichever is earlier), bars extrapolated backwards, to the last such event; to the end of the audio when the condition carries audio. The output's own first and last notes never move the span (B10, B13). Corpus references: first bar start to last event.
2. **Key segments.** A new key segment starts where the canonical BPM ratio to the previous musical segment leaves [1/τ, τ], default τ = 1.10: an exact octave joins, a fold jump from a 2.5% change breaks, 3:2 breaks. A passage never crosses a key-segment boundary; its canonical BPM is the duration-weighted mean of log canonical BPM over its segments. Fit split: one key segment in 88% of charts, two or more in 12.0% (13.4% at τ = 1.05, 10.6% at 1.20) **[measured]**.
3. **Cuts.** Nominal 16 canonical beats; each cut is the bar start nearest the previous cut plus 16, if within δ beats, else the exact beat; a final remainder shorter than μ beats merges into the previous passage, otherwise stands alone. Defaults δ = 2, μ = 4 (lengths mostly in [14, 20]); the census ran δ = 4, μ = 8 (lengths in [12, 24]); the structural results below do not depend on δ or μ. In 3/4 cuts fall on 15 or 18 beats (B3).
4. **Nothing dropped.** Every event lies in exactly one passage; a key segment shorter than μ is one short passage. Fit split at τ = 1.10: 306,165 passages, zero uncovered heads, zero charts without a passage (placeholder: 147); counts 4 at the 1st percentile, 7 at the 5th, 14 median, 37 at the 95th, 57 at the 99th; 19,505 passages (6.4%) under 8 beats, almost all slivers inside chains (10.0% at τ = 1.05, 4.0% at 1.20); 85 over 24 beats; 1,449 (0.47%) empty **[measured]**.
5. **Covariates**: actual length in canonical beats and seconds, canonical BPM, context availability on each side (chart edge, given, scored), scored fraction when a scope edge cuts it; report attributes (key-segment index, inside a chain, after a fold jump, holds a truncated bar) that are not key components.
6. **Scope edges (B12).** A scored span starting or ending inside a passage leaves the tiling alone; the passage is scored on the intersection, the scored length is the covariate, the given part is context. A given span inside a passage is never scored.
7. **Context reads (B11).** A family may read the neighbouring passages within the given or scored spans for state (holds entering, run length so far, the previous bar for recurrence); nothing is scored twice; an event's passage is where its head lies.

Cost: lengths vary within a song (longest over shortest 1.32 at the median chart, 1.50 at the 75th percentile with δ = 4, μ = 8, against 1.00 for fixed bars) **[measured]**, so the reference must condition on length (section 5); chains produce slivers whose beat-clock descriptions mean little. Both are carried by the covariate and by duration weights (section 6): a 0.4 s sliver contributes 4% of a 10 s passage, instead of a full vote or nothing.

**Continuity principle (proposed, to freeze).** The song statistic must be continuous in where the cuts fall: a passage's contribution goes to zero with its duration, and moving one cut by a bar changes at most two passages' weighted terms. The placeholder violates it (a dropped trailing block is a discontinuity of one passage). What would show the proposal wrong: E2 or E4 failing.

**On the bars decision.** Bars survive as alignment and attribution; the fixed count does not; I do not read this as revisiting [d-passage-bars](evaluation-first.md#d-passage-bars). One tension on record: [d-fold-per-segment](evaluation-first.md#d-fold-per-segment) makes a 158 to 162 song two key segments with different vocabularies and references. That is honest because canonical 81 is populated mostly by charts notated near 162 (two thirds of the corpus folds by −1, `r2-corpus.json` `dominant_fold`) **[notes]**; it is less efficient at the fold boundary where the kernel does not borrow for beat families. If E3 shows a cost, the remedy would be a continuity-preserving fold inside a key segment, which contradicts the decision and is parked, not proposed.

## 5. Proposal: what a passage is compared with <a id="p-key"></a>

(proposed) The reference is the cross-fitted fit-split passages, tiled by the same rule, weighted by a kernel over this key.

In the key: **requested star**, continuous (the source chart's star for corpus and injected charts, [d-star-key](operator-properties.md#d-star-key); for a partial scope still the whole chart's star, with the leniency of [le-label](operator-properties.md#failures) until a scoped difficulty condition exists); **canonical BPM of the passage**, kernel over its log, not circular, no borrowing across 80/160 for beat-clock families, intervals widening at the edges (the chart's dominant BPM is the wrong key for the 15.1% of charts with several); **the condition's fixed aspects**, so the model is of the free aspects given the fixed ones ([p-op-model](operator-properties.md#p-op-model)) and reference passages are scored under the same conditional; **scope shape**: scored length in beats (kernel), context availability per side, scored fraction. A whole chart's first passage has no left context and its reference is corpus first passages, which are intros; a continuation's first scored passage has given context on the left and its reference is fit passages cut at the same relative position. Position in the song thus enters only through what a family can read. Duration in seconds follows from BPM and length and is the covariate for seconds-clock families.

Not in the key: the realised star (decision); notated BPM or fold (not renotation-invariant; the renotation test enforces it); the song's passage count or duration (a choice of song, not of passage; if long songs' passages differ in kind, E5 by length band shows it as a failure to answer then); ranked or loved status; the mapper; segment kind (a rit passage is compared with steady passages at its BPM and length, and its weight keeps it from mattering). Position beyond context availability stays out until a diagnostic of calibration ranks against elapsed fraction shows intros or outros flagged.

Length conditioning makes the reference honest for short passages, which fixed lengths avoided by dropping them. Without a new model: each family's density takes length as an input where a description scales with it (counts, run maxima), and a surprisal's rank is taken among reference passages with kernel weights in (star, log canonical BPM, log length) and matching context availability; for scored fractions below one, reference passages are cut the same way. Effective sample size is reported with every rank; sparse keys widen intervals and never pull toward neutral ([p-op-divergence](operator-properties.md#p-op-divergence)). Fit strata: star 1 to 4 have 271 to 1,800 charts per canonical BPM band (189 to 1,316 song groups); star 5, 182 to 391; star 6, 77 to 134; star 7, 16 to 72; star 8, 2 to 27. Calibration: 15 to 25 charts per band at star 6, 3 to 15 at star 7, at most 1 at star 8 **[measured]**, so star 7 and above are reported as borrowed, never as calibrated.

## 6. Proposal: song aggregation and its null <a id="p-null"></a>

(proposed) Two views per scope, each with an empirical null that depends on scope duration smoothly, never on an exact passage count. Per passage: rank u_i against its reference, low and high tails.

**View 1, rate.** T = −Σ τ_i log u_i / Σ τ_i with τ_i the passage's duration in seconds (likewise on 1 − u_i for the high tail). The mean does not grow with passage count; the spread shrinks with total duration D = Σ τ_i. Seconds rather than canonical beats, because beats halve across a fold jump for the same music (B6) and the song as played is a span of time; E3's comparison column tests the choice.

**View 2, worst run.** S = max over runs of m consecutive passages, m in {1, 2, 4}, of the duration-weighted mean of −log u_i. It answers [le-whole-chart](operator-properties.md#failures): a song that met its LN ratio by stuffing one passage is normal in view 1 at large n and extreme in view 2. Fisher alone is blind to it: one passage at u = 10⁻⁶ adds 27.6 to −2 Σ log u, about 1.4 null standard deviations at n = 100 under independence and less under dependence **[estimate, arithmetic]**. The views combine through the empirical null of min(p_T, p_S), so the headline keeps one false-alarm rate ([fable-evaluator-blockers](fable-evaluator-blockers.md) cross-cutting 6).

**The null by scope shape.** (1) Whole-song scopes: the distribution of T and S over cross-fitted fit songs, kernel-weighted in (log D, star), one chart per song group per draw; a 90 s song at 3.4 stars borrows from 70 to 120 s songs at 3 to 4 stars with weights, and the effective number of null songs is reported; within-song dependence is in the null because the null is made of songs. (2) Partial scopes: the statistic over same-shape windows cut from fit songs at every bar position, same scored duration, same context geometry, same reads; a fit song of 30 passages yields about 22 windows of 8, dependent, so songs are the bootstrap unit. This serves one passage (the null is the rank itself, uniform if section 5 is honest), two adjacent passages (pairs carry their dependence), an edit of a few bars and a continuation alike, with as many null items as the fit split has positions and no need for songs of equal passage count. (3) Durations above the fit 95th percentile (290 s): Var(T) is approximately A(star)/D under stationary within-song dependence **[reasoning]**, A fitted by regressing (T − mean)² on 1/D over fit songs; the rank is taken against that normal law and marked as modelled; E5's long band checks it.

The brief's other options: an n-free statistic does not exist under dependence, the 1/D law is the next best thing and is used only where the corpus runs out; song random effects are in every empirical null by construction; block resampling within a song gives the song's own interval, not its p-value; subsampling passages from longer songs is what same-shape windows do; a per-passage false-alarm rate with a dependence correction is view 2. What would show the design wrong: calibration songs below rank 0.05 outside the binomial interval in any duration quintile or star band (E5), or single-passage ranks not uniform by length band (E4).

## 7. Injections and the invariance tests to add <a id="p-tests"></a>

Segmentation moves detection three ways **[reasoning]**: a defect straddling a cut is split (half dose per tile at stride 4; whole in some window at stride 1); a defect inside a sliver has almost no weight; a defect across a key-segment boundary is split across keys. Injections are therefore placed at a random bar phase under the sealed seed, so dose-response integrates over straddling, and one variant sits across a tempo change on purpose.

Tests to add, as hash comparisons of passage edges in ms where edges must not move and verdict comparisons against song-bootstrap noise where they may: per-segment renotation (edges identical); shift of chart and grid (identical after shifting back); time-stretch of both (identical in beats, scaled in seconds); an expressive line inserted, SV-style, short-bar or detour (identical); a redundant line on a bar start (identical) and mid-bar on a beat (edges may move at that bar; verdict within noise); a meter relabel without moving notes (within noise); the tiling anchor shifted by one to three bars (within noise, E2; trivial at stride 1); the owner's case, a tiny tempo change across the fold boundary (within noise and false-alarm rate unchanged, E3), and a 3% whole-song stretch from canonical 157 across 160; trailing silence appended (identical under a corpus-like span; under an audio condition the span extends and the outro is scored as empty passages at the corpus rate, to be documented as two behaviours, not a bug); first notes a beat before the first red line (scored like any other). The harness's (family × condition × scope) table gains the tiling parameters (τ, δ, μ, stride) per cell, so no sensitivity is quoted without its tiling.

## 8. Recommendation

**Freeze now.** Passages are a function of grid and scope only; the tiled span rule; key segments by canonical-BPM ratio; nominal 16 canonical beats cut at bar starts; nothing dropped; the covariates of section 4 item 5; the key of section 5 with its must-nots; duration weights in seconds; the two views and the same-shape empirical null; the tests of section 7; the continuity principle as a requirement on every later change to the tiling.

**Parameters with defaults.** τ = 1.10 (1.4 to 2.8 points of charts between 1.05 and 1.20); δ = 2, μ = 4; stride 4; kernel bandwidths by leave-one-group-out predictive likelihood on fit; scan runs {1, 2, 4}.

**Experiments.** All: fit cross-fitted by song group for references and nulls, calibration for the test, held-out untouched, one chart per song group by the sealed seed, song-group bootstrap, a fixed sample and a single run; a failed threshold is reported as failed.

<a id="pr-e2"></a>**E2, phase stability.** Claim: the anchor's phase does not decide a verdict. Data: 300 calibration song groups, each also with D1 (anchor by run length) and D4 (LN concentration) at the corpus-plausible dose and twice it, random phase. Metric: the standard deviation of the headline rank across anchor phases 0 to 3 bars over the song-bootstrap standard deviation. Threshold: median ratio below 0.5 at stride 4, below 0.1 at stride 1.

<a id="pr-e3"></a>**E3, fold-boundary stability.** Claim: a tempo change across the fold boundary does not change a normal song's verdict. Data: every calibration song with one key segment and canonical BPM in [150, 160) (about 200 **[estimate** from the 140 to 160 band]), second half stretched and relabelled by +1.5% (chart and grid together) so it folds near 80.5. Metrics: share of modified songs below headline rank 0.05; median absolute rank change against the original. Thresholds: share in [0.03, 0.08]; median change below 0.10. Reported beside: the same under canonical-beat weights. If both weightings fail, the result goes to the owner as a cost of [d-fold-per-segment](evaluation-first.md#d-fold-per-segment), with the continuity-preserving fold named.

<a id="pr-e4"></a>**E4, length-conditional honesty.** Claim: passage ranks are uniform whatever the length and shape. Data: all calibration passages as single-passage scopes, length bands under 8, 8 to 14, 14 to 18, 18 to 24 beats, shapes whole-chart tile, continuation's first scored passage, 8-bar edit. Metric: Kolmogorov distance from uniform per band and shape. Threshold: inside the 95% song-bootstrap band of the same distance on cross-fitted fit passages. A failure in the under-8 band alone means slivers are scored on the seconds clock only, a ledger-row change to section 4 item 4.

<a id="pr-e5"></a>**E5, song-null calibration.** Claim: the headline false-alarm rate is nominal in every band. Data: all calibration song groups, whole-song scope. Metric: share below headline rank 0.05 by duration quintile, star band 1 to 6, canonical BPM band, and the modelled band above 290 s. Thresholds: [0.03, 0.08] per quintile and star band (about 190 groups each); [0.02, 0.10] per BPM band for load families alone, the decision rule for a seconds segmentation of load (a failure organisation families do not share reopens it); [0.01, 0.12] for the long band (about 45 groups).

<a id="pr-e6"></a>**E6, stride.** Claim: stride 1 detects straddling defects at lower dose at equal false alarm. Data: D1 and D4 at five doses, random phase, 300 calibration groups, both strides. Metric: smallest dose at which at least 80% of injected songs fall below the calibration 5th percentile. Threshold: stride 1's dose at most 0.75 of stride 4's for at least one family; otherwise stride 4 stays.

<a id="pr-e7"></a>**E7, two views.** Claim: view 2 catches a single defective passage view 1 misses. Data: calibration songs with at least 20 passages, one passage injected with D4 at four times the corpus rate. Metric: detection at 5% false alarm under view 1, view 2 and the combined headline. Thresholds: view 2 at least 0.80, view 1 below 0.50, combined at least 0.75 with the combined false-alarm rate on unmodified songs in [0.03, 0.08]. If view 1 alone reaches 0.80, view 2 is dropped under property 7 ([p-op-evidence](operator-properties.md#p-op-evidence)).

**Order.** E4 and E5 first, since every other number is read against them; then E2 and E3, which are cheap; E6 and E7 once two families exist (anchor by run length and LN concentration suffice).

## 9. Rejected ideas

- Fixed count of bars as the measure of length: drops 6.1% of heads; four bars can be four red lines.
- Dropping passages under k notes or k beats: a cut-off by another name; covariates and weights instead.
- Event-count windows: boundaries become the generator's to move.
- Fixed-seconds windows for organisation: not time-stretch invariant for beat descriptions.
- A separate seconds tiling for load: not needed while E5 passes by BPM band.
- Phrase segmentation from the chart's notes (output-dependent) or from audio (the timing work's).
- Duration-adaptive bar counts: a key-dependent window without evidence of need.
- Exact passage-count strata for the song null: thin at both ends; kernel in log duration and same-shape windows instead.
- A theoretical χ² null for Fisher: wrong under within-song dependence.
- Within-song centring as the null: blind to a uniformly atypical song; kept as a dispersion family.
- Position in the song as a key component: sparsity for an effect not shown; diagnostic first.
- A continuity-preserving fold inside a song: contradicts [d-fold-per-segment](evaluation-first.md#d-fold-per-segment); parked behind E3.
- Keying by segment kind: a grid fact, not a mapper choice; a report attribute.
