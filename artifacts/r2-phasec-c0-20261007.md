- **Metric `ras-v1`.** Continuous five-state trace (four lanes, one overall; osu!mania decay bases 0.125 and
  0.30), charge dW(q) = w_I tau_I (x- . q + m/2) + w_G tau_G (g- m + m^2/2) with w_I = 1, w_G = 0.25, attacks
  only, taps and LN heads alike; W(S) over half-open [a, b); reference W_H from one tap per head row on the
  lane cycle 0, 2, 1, 3; r = sqrt(W/W_H). Head mask: free lane with code 1 or 2, held lane with code 3 or 4.
- **Part 1, code:** new `r2/strain.py`, one optional bias argument on `sampling.continue_chart` (identity
  required bit for bit), `tests/r2/test_strain.py` with twelve mechanical tests, a driver `r2/c0.py`.
  Overhead of the trace in sampling measured against a 5% target.
- **Part 2, natural r on fit_dev sources:** scopes of 2/4/8/16/32/60 s, admission at least 8 head rows,
  quantiles of r, W/|S|, W_H/|S|, with a greedy single-tap floor and an all-chord ceiling (both ignoring holds).
- **Part 3, on the newest phase-N checkpoint (no training):** (a) fixed-eta scan, eta in {-4..4}, 16
  anchors x 4/16 s x 2 seeds; (b) the analytic budget controller (Astra's counterfactual teacher used
  directly: reference-clock allocation, per-decision eta by bisection, clipped by |eta| <= 8 and a KL cap
  kappa in {0.25, 1} nats), targets at the natural 10th/50th/90th percentile of r per length plus one
  outside the range, 8 anchors x 2/4/8/16 s x 2 seeds, plus phase-N scopes. Readouts: attainment in log r,
  chords, same-lane repeats, LN share, KL and phase-N likelihood (departure), charge profile (terminal burst).
  At most 2 CPU threads, so phase N is not slowed.
- **Part 4, blind-screen pairs:** 48 pairs of 8-16 s scopes on the same skeleton and prefix (tilted
  +/-, two natural samples with different r, constructed mechanical contrasts, near-equal controls),
  exported for the beatmap-lens harness, sides randomised, key sealed in `screen/KEY-sealed.json`. Judges:
  the human and a separate Astra thread; the job that builds the pairs does not judge.
- **Out of scope:** any training, LN or style work, the phase-C configs, star or `baseline.py`.

Why the analytic controller before a network: if a parameter-free controller on the 15 mask costs cannot
attain requests at acceptable departure, training a network to imitate it is premature (agent suggestion in
[a-strain-proxy-reading](r2-phasec-frontier-20261007.md#a-strain-proxy-reading), kept as a design choice
of this brief, not a human decision).
