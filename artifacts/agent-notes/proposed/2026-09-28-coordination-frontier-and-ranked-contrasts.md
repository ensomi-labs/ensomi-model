## Hypothesis: demand is the effort of continuing an organization

The candidate abstraction is a coordination trajectory constrained by the chart:
from the same committed state, how much reorganization, rapid movement reversal
and sustained concentration does each legal future require? Stable high coverage
can be economical; replacing it by frequent staggered entry/exit changes can
require more coordination even at equal head rate and equal rating.

The canonical V3 profile fixes fingering and symmetry. Internal coordination
states would describe task demand, not individual fatigue, pain or measured
biomechanics. Exact occupancy remains replay fact. Latent hand/bilateral dynamics
and a cost-to-go over actual future events are possible representations; their
response comparisons must be established independently before they become a
frontier model.

Closest motor-control analogues:
[Haken, Kelso and Bunz](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00336922) model coordination
transitions as changes in coupled dynamics, rather than independent event costs;
[Todorov and Jordan](https://www.nature.com/articles/nn963) formulate coordination
through task constraints and feedback cost. Only those principles transfer.
Their experimental tasks and fitted parameters are not a calibrated 4K profile.
This is a provisional adaptation, not a novel motor theory or validated simulator.

A useful prediction is that coordination changes in easier ranked passages have
different available timing/occupancy context from apparently similar transitions
in harder or generated passages. Another is that the same future can be more or
less demanding after different organized histories, despite matching scalar
load. Mere corpus rarity or row surprise cannot be substituted for this demand.
