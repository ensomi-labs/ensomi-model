## Hypothesis: demand is the effort of continuing an organization

The candidate abstraction is a coordination trajectory constrained by the chart:
from the same committed state, how much reorganization, rapid movement reversal
and sustained concentration does each legal future require? Stable high coverage
can be economical; replacing it by frequent staggered entry/exit changes can
require more coordination even at equal head rate and equal rating.

The canonical V3 profile fixes fingering and symmetry. Internal coordination
states would describe task demand, not individual fatigue, pain or measured
biomechanics. Exact occupancy remains replay fact. Latent hand/bilateral dynamics
and a cost-to-go over actual future events are possible representations; their
response comparisons must be established independently before they become a
frontier model.

Closest motor-control analogues:
[Haken, Kelso and Bunz](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00336922) model coordination
transitions as changes in coupled dynamics, rather than independent event costs;
[Todorov and Jordan](https://www.nature.com/articles/nn963) formulate coordination
through task constraints and feedback cost. Only those principles transfer.
Their experimental tasks and fitted parameters are not a calibrated 4K profile.
This is a provisional adaptation, not a novel motor theory or validated simulator.

A useful prediction is that coordination changes in easier ranked passages have
different available timing/occupancy context from apparently similar transitions
in harder or generated passages. Another is that the same future can be more or
less demanding after different organized histories, despite matching scalar
load. Mere corpus rarity or row surprise cannot be substituted for this demand.

## Architecture and learning recommendation

Recommendation REFINE. Model continuation as task-constrained extension of
an established coordination trajectory, preserving both sustained execution
and reorganization. Regularity alone cannot reward a long-jack failure;
rarity or low lane entropy alone cannot condemn a legitimate motif.

Keep exact replay separately. Compare a chart-only state with continuous-time
flow under actual occupancy and noncommutative complete-row updates; allow
finger, within-hand and bilateral interactions with mirror-equivariant
parameters. A stable linear/oscillator flow is a simple candidate, not a
physiological law or imposed beat grid. A fuller elapsed-time sequence reader
can first establish response distinctions and then guide state compression.
Neural CDE is a representation analogue:
https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.08926. No existing neural hidden coordinate becomes
a demand unit merely by being named after coordination.

Treat frontier2 as actor energy unless independently calibrated. The complete
continuation response must receive actual hypothetical future events and a
real time horizon. R currently lacks general TAP-layout history; a declared
committed coordination summary or full-branch selection can supply the missing
dependency without transferring row cardinality/columns to skeleton. Changing
the neural H dependency is a separate optional comparison. Whole-future
selection must be able to revise uncommitted H/R when every row realization
is inappropriate; row-only energy cannot undo an already selected pure-R
time, especially with singleton legal row support.

The preferred next training line is a clean joint proposal comparison, with
the inherited endpoint retained as baseline: same architecture/actual law
and broad factual recipe, compare current, pre-scalar-outcome and fresh
initializations across sufficient budget curves. Separate explicit-condition
balancing from unknown-control prior. Independently calibrate future responses;
corpus trajectory learning and real/generated discrimination can aid proposal
fidelity but do not supply physiological labels. Source histories retain their
own factual suffixes; generated histories use their actual outcomes and are
refreshed from the current BOS policy.

The report also isolates a conditional-survival hypothesis for fragmented

(1-release probability). More H opportunities can shorten real duration
without changing R1 parameters if per-opportunity release behavior transfers

or that constant hazard has been measured. A proposed evaluation stratifies
source risk sets by real age, H density, held roles and control, retains both
H-atom and pure-R releases, and handles crop censoring. It can distinguish
opportunity density from persistence preference without rewarding a universal
LN length. A different survival parameterization remains a hypothesis.
