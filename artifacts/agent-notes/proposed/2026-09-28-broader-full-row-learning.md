## Mechanism and decision

The 256-window full-R1 fit improved Classic/STYX amount behavior and both Stream
attack responses; count-only and missing-history alternatives did not establish
better native quality. Lens now confirms mixed LN/TAP organization in both
Classic seeds but continuing near-all-LN flow in Blizzard, without the reference
anchor/TAP relationships. STYX's amount improvement is not a demonstrated local
organization repair. The difficulty response probe also rejects a uniform -1
neural-star adjustment as a generally calibrated solution.

The selected next intervention is broader factual exposure of the actual
history/layout/release/count owners, not another isolated quantity readout or a
new shape filter. It tests whether the promising 128-update parent was simply
underexposed under the repaired recipe. The closest analogue is ordinary
continued maximum-likelihood learning with a declared sampling distribution;
there is no novelty claim. The alternative remains that native state conditions,
timing structure or missing continuation supervision dominate and further source
learning will not help. This run can reject scaling this particular recipe.
