## Question and design boundary

Short-LN-dominated specialist charts are valid corpus modes, not the ordinary
default. An empirical distribution of modes is different from averaging their
per-head preferences. At fixed source H, the current model's Stream request
still produces51LN among52heads in[18335,23335), with130ms median duration.
Teacher-state LN-visibility/scope probes have small effects; changing only H
does not repair this autonomous distribution.

A new R1 plan should make a correlated choice shared by several decisions,
rather than equating a requested ratio with a per-prefix quota. It belongs
inside R1, which continues to own counts, columns, TAP/LN births and releases.
H continues to propose times and cannot decide finger roles. The pressure
frontier evaluates prospective gameplay and does not assign musical styles.

Two different questions must not be conflated: whether the present decoder
can express organized TAP when given a persistent composition condition, and
whether such a condition contains enough information for held roles and motifs.
A single LN fraction cannot represent the latter; even success in the small
probe below would justify a richer joint plan, not certify it as implemented.

Closest analogues are MusicVAE's subsequence-level latent decoder
(https://proceedings.mlr.press/v80/roberts18a.html) and ACT's action-sequence
prediction (https://tonyzhaozh.github.io/aloha/). The transferable primitive is
a shared future-action choice. Their independent subsequences/continuous-action
averaging are not applicable to exact four-key occupancy, coupled integer rows,
and open LNs across publication/control boundaries. This is an adaptation,
not a novelty claim. The previous whole-song four-code prototype and failed
missing-history/MMD/memory studies remain relevant negative evidence.
