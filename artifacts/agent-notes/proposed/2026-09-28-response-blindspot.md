## Mechanism and alternatives

The independent response uses four exponential memories and reciprocal HH/RH/HR
impulses. Its added-work potential is identically zero below each marginal
99th-percentile source maximum. At D4 the fastest HR reference is17.43895;
an isolated25ms release adds16 to that coordinate, below the dead zone. Other
coordinates and slower scales must be checked through the actual functions
before claiming a completely free transition.

A graded quadratic potential has positive response throughout the same state
space. This is a control-theoretic storage-function alternative, not a claim of
novel physiology. It may restore local ordering but still fail after scalar
pooling and permissive source-max calibration. Corpus rarity, source mode
mixtures and actor proposal learning are distinct questions. Hard minimum-LN
rules or deleting legitimate short-LN source examples are not interventions.
