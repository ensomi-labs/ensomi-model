## Design [P]

- **Inputs.** Per row, the arrangement is the one-hot of the four lane decision codes (4 × 5, R2's target space) plus the four held-before bits from exact replay. The rows are given as gap-before and gap-after categories in a fitted alphabet of log2(gap / chart tatum), phase within the grid beat (12 classes) and beat index mod 4. The tatum is the chart's modal beat gap. Nothing is in milliseconds.
- **Encoded:** the arrangement given the rows, at log2 K bits per window: chord classes, LN heads and releases, same-lane and same-hand recurrence over neighbouring rows, one-hand against split-hand, placement in the window's rhythm.
- **Indistinguishable by construction:**
  - left from right: the row embedding is f(x) + f(μx), so z(μx) = z(x), and the loss takes the better orientation per window, never per row, so the hand difference is kept;
  - tempo and milliseconds;
  - row count and duration: mean pooling per window, and summaries free of length.
  - Blindness to the rows is an information-bottleneck argument (the decoder already has the rows) and is measured, not assumed.
- **Architecture.** Symmetrised linear row embedding plus rows embeddings → 3 residual Conv1d → mean pool per window of W grid beats → EMA codebook (K codes, decay 0.99, β 0.25, dead-code restarts) → decoder: code plus rows embedding per row → 3 residual Conv1d → 4 × 5 logits. Non-autoregressive, so the codes must carry what the rows do not. 132k parameters at K 64, d 32 [M].
- **Unit.** One code per W grid beats, W ∈ {1, 2, 4}: metrical, because corpus repetition is metrical [M-design]; powers of two so the grid's factor-2 fold stays inside the candidate set. A section is a code sequence, summarised as a code histogram plus repeat rate (bigrams at the full run), each against null codes drawn i.i.d. from the pool's marginal at the section's own length.
- **Model selection, label-free.** K ∈ {32, 64, 128, 256}, W ∈ {1, 2, 4}, d ∈ {16, 32, 64}, two seeds, 10 % of song groups held out. Criteria: held-out NLL and its gain over permuted codes and over a rows-only decoder; usage and perplexity; two-seed agreement after Hungarian matching. Lens labels take no part.
- **Evaluation.** Design §6 splits (D, V1, batch swap, V2). Per concept, absent against present within terciles of the owning level fitted on D. Logistic probes fitted on D. Decision statistic: ΔAUC = AUC(mass + codes) − AUC(mass), song-group bootstrap 95 % interval above 0 on V1, sign held in the swap, and null codes not reaching it. Comparison with the spaces is coded and waits for `artifacts/r2-spaces-20261008/sections.parquet`.
- **Would show failure:** usage collapse; NLL with codes close to permuted or rows-only; rows leakage; seed agreement near chance; ΔAUC intervals containing 0 for every concept or flipping in the swap; LN carried only by held mass.
- **Relation to S1-S8 [I].** The codes are a joint learned coarse-graining that mixes S1, S3, S4, S5 and S6 at the window scale and separates no owner. They are the comparison arm: mass + spaces + codes against mass + spaces says whether the alphabet sees anything the owners miss. A code n-gram model is a candidate for S7's familiar-pattern model.

## Proposed full run, for the human's check [P]

- **Data:** all fit_train charts outside the 1,032 Lens validation groups (about 11,000), 10 % of groups held out, 32-beat windows.
- **Sweep:** K {32, 64, 128, 256} × W {1, 2, 4} at d 32, then d {16, 64} at the chosen (K, W). 14 configurations × 2 seeds × 3,000 steps, plus a rows-only decoder once per W.
- **Final:** the selected configuration, 2 seeds × 20,000 steps, plus rows-only.
- **Mac compute,** at the smoke rate (0.04 s per step): sweep about 1 h, selected runs 27 min, rows-only 13 min, evaluation and code atlas 3 min. About 1 h 45 min in total, splittable into sweep and final.
- **Read-outs:** the selection table; for the chosen configuration, V1, swap and V2 with the null control, blindness, seed agreement, the comparison with the spaces, and a code atlas.
- **Additions from the smoke run:** probe C chosen on D by grouped cross-validation (the only label-dependent choice); summaries compared at matched length or smoothed with a corpus-fitted strength.

## Questions for the human (each changes the design)

1. **Should the encoder see the rows features at all?** With them, codes can express "chord on an off-beat", which makes them richer, but that is also the path for rows leakage. Without them (`--no-enc-rows`), blindness holds by construction.
2. **Code per grid beat, or per 2 or 4 beats with n-grams carrying the rest?** The sweep decides by corpus criteria only. If the unit should match a musical phrase instead, W is fixed rather than swept.
3. **The supervised variant** (concept heads on D, validated on V1 and V2) is proposed, not coded. Build it only if the unsupervised codes fail the concept check.
