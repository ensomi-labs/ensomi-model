## Qualification is execution, then independent judgment

`run_qualification` and its packaged Hydra entrypoint generate from BOS,
verify exports, evaluate separately declared scopes and record actual publication
traces. A numerical failure keeps completed outputs and blocks promotion.
An otherwise clear candidate is still `review_required`, with pending semantic
review and promotion false. Successful execution is not a qualified model.
Older qualification paths do not automatically invoke the optional planner;
check the actual session class and policy options for the result being read.

LN-total gates require a matching completed request extent. A whole-song
request interrupted by an override does not impose separate total quotas on
the preceding and restored fragments. Difficulty/occupation/pressure observations
remain separate there. Scope corrections must preserve original results and
explain the changed assessment rather than overwrite inconvenient evidence.

Whole-chart stars, local strain proxies, source difficulty metadata and a
player-response model are not interchangeable. In particular, the H star bound
belongs to the named 20241007 whole-chart algorithm. Comparing it to individual
pieces of a changing control schedule would be invalid.

Publication claims must identify their clock: cached Mel/loaded weights,
fresh audio/API readiness, or process-start readiness. The two-second qualifier
and the separate benchmark's thirty-row/eight-second startup contract are
different. None includes a shipped client's complete rendering/network behavior.
Preserve incomplete prefixes and actual open holds on failure.
