# Formulation view

Each problem-map node read against the V3 formulation: what the formulation fixes, what it names and leaves undefined, and where the notes supply evidence the formulation does not yet absorb. The formulation is product documentation, cited at primary commit `4a8a478` (`git show 4a8a478:docs/formulation/README.md`). It owns definitions; these notes do not redefine them.

| Node | What the formulation fixes | What it leaves open | Evidence in the notes |

[Evaluation and its limits](../artifacts/evaluation-and-evidence-boundaries.md) |
