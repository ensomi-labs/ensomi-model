## Lessons that outlived their experiments

<a id="l-execution-is-not-quality"></a>**Execution and quality are different achievements.** Exact replay and export, a legitimate joint likelihood and fast publication do not establish musical organisation. [The evaluation account](../artifacts/evaluation-and-evidence-boundaries.md) separates mechanical checks, native diagnostics, read images and human judgment.

<a id="l-timing-and-rows-couple"></a>**Timing and rows constrain each other without sharing every responsibility.** Required head times set unavoidable attack load; rows can still misuse valid times through chord, release and column choices. Source-timing substitutions do not allocate a percentage of blame. A release penalty applied only after a forced release event may come too late to prefer waiting.

<a id="l-available-is-not-effective"></a>**Available information is not necessarily effective information.** Shared additive conditions can cancel from relative odds, a constant latent can block plan inputs, and accepted consequence tensors can go unused. In the other direction, architectural access or a nonzero gradient does not prove native use sufficient for quality.

<a id="l-distribution-includes-missingness"></a>**The training distribution includes support, sampling and missingness.** Hard recovery exclusions remove real supervision. Balancing rare LN cases and then hiding the request changes the default prior. Annotation extents and the states the current policy reaches matter. Real suffix labels cannot be reused after inventing a different prefix.

<a id="l-response-must-see-the-distinction"></a>**A response must see the distinction it is asked to enforce.** Attack counts, star values, LN fractions and marginal occupancy can agree while coordination differs. More candidate samples cannot help a selector that gives all relevant alternatives equal cost. Corpus rarity is not physiological truth, and expressive ranked counterexamples must stay inspectable.
